IPTV is a technology that allows users to access digital television content over the internet. Instead of using a traditional cable or satellite subscription, IPTV allows users to access a wide range of TV channels, radio stations, and on-demand content through their internet connection. It is an increasingly popular alternative to traditional cable or satellite TV as it is usually more affordable and offers more flexibility.
